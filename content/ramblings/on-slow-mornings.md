*Sample post.* There's a particular kind of quiet before anyone else is awake. The kettle is the loudest thing in the house, and for about forty minutes the day hasn't decided what it is yet.

I've started protecting that time. No phone until the coffee's gone. It turns out most of the things I "had to check" could wait until 8.
